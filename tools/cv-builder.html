① Profile Builder

  1. Fill in your details section by section. Fields marked * are required.
  2. Click 💡 Show example on any section to see a filled-in example (Steve Smith, fictional) beside your own fields.
  3. Everything saves automatically. Click Export CV Bank (.txt) to download a copy of your profile, or Use in Prompt Builder to go straight to Step 2.

✨ Polish with AI

  1. When your profile is done, click Copy polish prompt at the bottom of the Profile Builder. It copies an editing prompt with your whole CV Bank attached.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI chat.
  3. Paste the AI's reply into the box and click Check & Import. The tool compares it line by line with what you sent and warns you before anything important (headings, links, names, dates, figures) is changed.

② Prompt Builder

  1. Paste the job description into the big box. It saves automatically.
  2. Click ⎘ Copy Cover Letter Prompt or ⎘ Copy CV Prompt. Your CV Bank and the job description are injected into the template and the finished prompt is copied in one step. The button turns green once copied.
  3. Paste it into Claude.

Customising the prompt templates

  • Open Cover Letter Prompt or CV / LaTeX Prompt under Your prompt templates to edit it. The badge switches to customised.
  • Your edits save automatically: to your account when you're signed in, or in this browser when you're not.
  • ↺ Reset to default brings back the original template. ⬇ Download .txt and 📤 Import .txt let you keep a copy as a file.

③ Export to Word

  1. Copy Claude's entire reply (it contains tagged sections like [SALUTATION] or [SUMMARY]).
  2. Pick the matching document type, then click Read from clipboard. If your browser blocks clipboard access, use paste manually.
  3. Check the preview. Click any text to edit it, add or remove bullet points and rows, and pick a colour and font. Your edits are saved.
  4. Click Download .docx. Your name, contact details and links come from the Profile Builder.

Signature

  • Upload a photo or scan of your signature for cover letters. Any background works: white, grey or textured paper.
  • The tool removes the background, cleans up specks and crops it tightly, so only your ink appears on the letter.

Your data

  • Signed in with Google: your profile, job description, prompts, document edits, style and signature save to your account automatically and follow you to any device. Only you can access them.
  • Not signed in: everything saves in this browser only. Sign in later and it moves into your account.
  • Signing out removes your data from this browser. It stays safe in your account.
