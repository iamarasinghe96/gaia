① Profile Builder

  1. Fill in your details section by section. Fields marked * are required.
  2. Click 💡 Show example on any section to see a filled-in example (Steve Smith, fictional) beside your own fields.
  3. Import from CV fills your profile for you: choose a PDF or Word CV and the AI copies your details into the right fields (sign in first), or choose a CV Bank (.txt) you exported earlier to load it exactly, with no AI.
  4. Everything saves automatically. Click Export CV Bank (.txt) to download a copy of your profile.

✨ Spelling & grammar check

  1. When your profile is done, click Check spelling & use in Prompt Builder at the bottom of the Profile Builder.
  2. If you're signed in, the AI fixes spelling, grammar and punctuation, saves your profile and takes you to the Prompt Builder. It keeps your own words and never changes names of people, places or organisations, in any language, or your dates, links and figures.
  3. A note at the top of the Prompt Builder says how many fixes were made. Click See changes to check them or Undo to put your original text back.
  4. If the AI is busy, your profile is left as it is and you still go straight to the Prompt Builder. Try again in a minute.

Your profile text is sent securely to Groq to be proofread. Gaia Symbiosis does not store it.

② Prompt Builder

  1. Paste the job description into the big box. It saves automatically.
  2. Click ⎘ Copy Cover Letter Prompt or ⎘ Copy CV Prompt. Your CV Bank and the job description are injected into the template and the finished prompt is copied in one step. The button turns green once copied.
  3. Paste it into Claude.

Customising the prompt templates

  • Open Cover Letter Prompt or CV / LaTeX Prompt under Your prompt templates to edit it. The badge switches to customised.
  • Your edits save automatically: to your account when you're signed in, or in this browser when you're not.
  • ↺ Reset to default brings back the original template. ⬇ Download .txt and 📤 Import .txt let you keep a copy as a file.

③ Export to Word

  1. Copy Claude's entire reply (it contains tagged sections like [SALUTATION] or [SUMMARY]) then click Paste Cover Letter Reply or Paste CV Reply in the Prompt Builder, right under the copy buttons. The tool reads it from your clipboard.
  2. Paste the cover letter reply, then copy the CV prompt, run it and paste that reply too. Once both are in, Export to Word opens. Switch between them with Cover Letter and CV above the preview.
  3. Check the preview. Click any text to edit it, add or remove bullet points and rows, and pick a colour and font. Your edits are saved.
  4. Click Download .docx, or Both for the two documents. Your name, contact details and links come from the Profile Builder.
  5. In Chrome or Edge on a computer, the first download offers to use a folder for your job applications. Each application is then saved in Company › Position, read from Claude's reply, and an existing company folder is reused. Check or change both names under Save to a folder. The folder stays on your computer and is never uploaded.

Signature

  • Upload a photo or scan of your signature in Profile Builder › Personal Information, next to your headshot. Any background works: white, grey or textured paper.
  • The tool removes the background, cleans up specks and crops it tightly, so only your ink appears on the letter.

🔒 Your data

  • Signed in with Google: your profile, job description, prompts, document edits, style and signature save to your account and follow you to any device. Security rules let only your account read or change them.
  • Not signed in: everything stays in this browser only. Sign in later and it moves into your account.
  • Signing out removes your data from this browser. It stays safe in your account.
  • We never sell your data or use it for advertising. Read the full Privacy Policy.
