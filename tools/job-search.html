How it works

Three steps from search to application.

  1. Step 1

    Tell us what you do

    Describe your work in your own words or import your CV. AI picks the job titles and keywords employers use.

  2. Step 2

    See sponsor jobs first

    Live ads are matched to you and checked against the sponsor list. Ads that say "citizens only" are flagged.

  3. Step 3

    Build your tailor-made CV for “Job Position”

    One click sends the job to the CV Prompt Builder, with your profile already filled in from your CV.

Next: your tailor-made CV for “Job Position”.

Every match has a "Build my CV for this job" button. It opens the CV Prompt Builder with the job ready and your profile filled in.
